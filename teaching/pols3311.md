POLS 3311. University of Houston, Fall 2026.

This course offers an introduction to theoretical and empirical research in
comparative politics, a field that seeks to explain variation in political
institutions and outcomes across countries, and variation in political behavior
across individuals and groups within them. We will study the concept of state and
its formation, political regimes, constitutional design, electoral rules and
political parties, focusing throughout on how institutions shape the policies
governments adopt and the outcomes those policies produce. Making sense of these
explanations requires understanding the nature of power: who holds it, how it is
exercised, and what constrains it.
