Welcome to my website

I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Houston. I was previously an Assistant Professor in the Division of the Humanities and Social Sciences at the California Institute of Technology, and a Postdoctoral Associate at the Cowles Foundation for Research in Economics at Yale University. I received my Ph.D. from Princeton University, where I was affiliated with the Research Program in Political Economy and was awarded the Charlotte Elizabeth Procter Fellowship.

I study how collective decisions get made — inside committees, where a few people deliberate and vote in sequence, and at the ballot box, where voters hold politicians to account. My work joins formal models to data using structural estimation, field and laboratory experiments, and text analysis, drawing on the Federal Open Market Committee, the U.S. Supreme Court, and elections in the United States, Mexico, and the Philippines. A related methodological strand develops tools for scaling votes and speech into common measures of political positions.
